🧨 Safe Sweep

  1. Don’t tap on the cells that hide bombs.
  2. Tapping a safe cell shows how many bombs surround it.
  3. Clear the field to reveal the code.
  4. Tip: Right‑click (desktop) or long‑press (mobile) to place a 🚩 flag.

💥 Diffuser Protocol

  1. Place 7 diffusers on the 7×7 board.
  2. No two diffusers can share a row or a column.
  3. Diffusers cannot touch — not even diagonally.
  4. Each colored region must contain exactly one diffuser.

🛰️ Micro‑Maze Escape

  1. Guide the probe from Start (cyan) to Exit (green).
  2. Tap a neighboring cell or use arrow keys to move.
  3. Use New Maze for a fresh layout.

💎 Jewel Shift

  1. Swipe (mobile) or use arrow keys to slide jewels.
  2. Matching jewels merge into a higher tier.
  3. Reach the target tier to proceed.
