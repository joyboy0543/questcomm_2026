🧨 Safe Sweep

  1. Don’t tap on the cells that hide bombs.
  2. Tapping a safe cell shows how many bombs surround it.
  3. Clear the field to proceed.
  4. Tip: Right‑click (desktop) or long‑press (mobile) to place a 🚩 flag, if you suspect a bomb is under the cell.

💥 Diffuser Protocol

  1. Place 7 diffusers on the 7×7 board.
  2. No two diffusers can share a row or a column.
  3. Diffusers cannot touch — not even diagonally.
  4. Each colored region must contain exactly one diffuser.

💎 Locate the Jewel

  1. Swipe (mobile) or use arrow keys (desktop) to slide jewels.
  2. When two identical jewels collide, they merge into the next tier.
  3. Goal: Reach the target tier Amethyst to proceed.
