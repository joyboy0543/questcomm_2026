How to Brew a basic Padlock Potion

  • To bind a common padlock shut and command its clasp to sleep.
  • First, one eyelash from a wild dragon - caught in the sunset’s ruby‑sparked glow.
  • Next, two bones from a royal frog - crown‑kissed and pale as snow.
  • Last, three drops of snot from a liar’s nose - lies condensed to a glistening flow.
  • Stir clockwise: once for the lash, twice for the bones, thrice for the drops.
  • Then whisper: “By scale and crown, by truth denied - bolt, bar, and latch, be tied.”
